They Live: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

They Live ("the game") is a free mobile game for Android and iPhone. This policy explains what information is collected when you play it and why.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts and does not send your progress, settings or any personal information to us. Your best stage, best score and settings are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game is free because it shows ads, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps), and diagnostic information such as crash data.

Depending on your choices and where you live, this information may be used to show personalized ads. Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy Choices on the game's title screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone, the game asks before any tracking, and you can change that under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads in the game are limited to content rated for teens. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data security and sharing

We do not sell, share or receive any personal information. Information collected by Google's ad SDK is sent over encrypted connections and handled by Google as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
